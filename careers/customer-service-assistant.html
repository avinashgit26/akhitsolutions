Customer Service Assistant.

Help us deliver a great experience. Join our team and make a real difference for our customers.

Role overview

Help us deliver a great experience. Join our team and make a real difference for our customers. You’ll work with a collaborative team that values clarity, quality and practical solutions.

Key responsibilities

  • Respond to customer enquiries by email, phone and other channels in a professional and timely manner.
  • Help resolve customer issues and follow up to ensure a good outcome.
  • Keep accurate records of customer interactions.
  • Work with the wider team to improve the customer experience.
  • Escalate complex issues when needed.

Key requirements

  • Good communication skills, both written and verbal.
  • A positive and helpful approach.
  • Good attention to detail.
  • Ability to manage multiple enquiries.
  • Willingness to learn and work as part of a team.
  • Previous customer service experience is desirable but not essential.
