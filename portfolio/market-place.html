The project

A flexible commerce platform for a wider market.

Market Place is a multi-vendor e-commerce platform that brings together buyers and sellers in a clean, intuitive and reliable environment.

Designed around discovery and trust.

We focused on a seamless shopping experience with clear product discovery, straightforward journeys and responsive behaviour across devices.

Outcome

A simpler way to manage complex work.

The product brings important workflows into one clear system, reducing manual hand-offs and giving teams a more reliable view of what is happening now and what needs attention next.
